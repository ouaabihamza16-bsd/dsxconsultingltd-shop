About us

Practical management support, without big-firm overhead.

DSX CONSULTING LTD is an independent management consultancy based in Guildford, Surrey. The company was incorporated on 13 February 2024 and is registered in England and Wales under company number 15486856.

We work with owners and managers who want an objective outside view of how their organisation is run. That might mean sharpening priorities and the plan behind them, simplifying the way work gets done, or keeping an important change on track.

The emphasis is on clear thinking and workable results: an honest diagnosis, plans written in plain English, and hands-on help putting them into practice.

Services

Where we can help

Four areas of management consultancy, each shaped around your organisation and the problem in front of you.

Strategy & Planning

Helping leadership teams clarify their priorities and turn them into a practical, measurable plan.

  • Clarifying objectives and priorities
  • Turning goals into a practical plan
  • Simple measures to track progress

Operations & Process Improvement

Reviewing how work gets done and removing waste, bottlenecks and duplication.

  • Mapping how work currently flows
  • Finding bottlenecks and duplicated effort
  • Designing simpler, clearer processes

Projects & Change

Supporting the planning, governance and delivery of change initiatives so they land on time.

  • Scoping and planning change initiatives
  • Proportionate governance and reporting
  • Hands-on support through delivery

Management Advisory

An independent sounding board for owners and managers on organisational and operational decisions.

  • An objective outside perspective
  • Structuring options before a decision
  • Organisational and operational questions
